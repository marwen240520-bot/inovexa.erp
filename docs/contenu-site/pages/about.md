Our Story

# Business software built in Tunis for companies everywhere

Inovexa was founded in Tunis in 2024 to give growing businesses the kind of ERP that used to be reserved for large corporations: finance, HR, supply chain, sales, and analytics in one cloud platform, at a price a small or mid-sized company can justify.

Founded in Tunis, 2024 Serving 12+ countries Part of MSAI Systems

Our Mission

## Serious ERP at a price growing businesses can afford

Growing companies need what large ones need: accurate books, payroll that runs on time, stock figures they can trust, and reports they can act on. We build those tools as modules, so a business can start with what it needs today and add more as it grows, wherever it operates.

Our Vision

## One ERP that works in any country

A company in Tunis, Toronto, or Nairobi should be able to run the same platform with its own tax rules, currency, and language. That is why every module we ship supports multiple countries, currencies, and languages from the start instead of having them added later.

What We Stand For

## Our Core Values

The principles we use when we decide what to build, how to price it, and how to support customers.

### Useful AI

We add AI where it saves measurable time, such as demand forecasting and spotting unusual transactions, and leave it out where it does not.

### Clear Pricing and Terms

No hidden fees and no surprise contracts. You know what you are getting, what it costs, and what is on our roadmap.

### Built for Real Teams

Teams work in French, Spanish, or English, and screens are designed for warehouse and shop-floor staff as well as accountants.

### Less Hardware

Cloud deployment replaces on-premise servers that run around the clock, so there is less hardware to buy, power, and maintain.

Our Story

## From Tunisia to the World

Inovexa was born in 2024 in Tunis. Our founders had spent years watching Tunisian manufacturers, distributors, and growing companies struggle with fragmented tools: Excel spreadsheets, disconnected billing software, and manual HR processes. The problem wasn't a lack of ambition. It was a lack of the right tools.

We asked a simple question: why does a factory in Sfax have to use 1990s-era software while a startup in San Francisco gets AI-native ERP on day one? The answer was always cost and complexity. So we decided to solve both.

Today, Inovexa is part of [MSAI Systems](https://msai-systems.com) and serves businesses in more than 12 countries from its base in Tunis. The goal has not changed: the depth of a large ERP, with the setup time and price of a modern SaaS product.
