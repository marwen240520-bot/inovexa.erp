## Tax rules and currencies for every country you sell in

Inovexa ERP supports custom tax rules and currencies for virtually every country.

### Custom Tax Engine

Configure any tax rule: Tunisian TVA, European VAT, UK VAT, US sales tax, withholding taxes, excise duties, and country-specific exemptions. Update rates without code changes.

### 180+ Currencies

Multi-currency invoicing, real-time exchange rates, and consolidated reporting in any currency. Invoice in TND, EUR, USD, GBP, SAR, AED, and any other currency your customers require.

### Country-Specific Compliance

Pre-configured for local fiscal rules, e-invoicing mandates, and statutory reporting formats in dozens of countries. New jurisdictions added continuously based on customer demand.
